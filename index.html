Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all applications published by Hezarfen (Onour Impram) on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

Covered Applications:

  • DogPulse: Dog Care & Health Tracker
  • ReptileLog: Reptile Care & Morph Calculator

1. Data Collection and Storage

All your personal data (pet profiles, care records, feeding logs, health records, reminders, and settings) is stored locally on your device using a SQLite database. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers.

Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it using the app's backup/export feature.

2. Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Sentry for crash reporting and error tracking. This service collects anonymous technical data (device type, OS version, crash logs) solely to improve app stability. No personal information or user content is included in crash reports.

3. Advertising

Our free tier displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers (Advertising ID) for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Premium subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

4. In-App Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. Payment information is handled entirely by Google/Apple. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment details.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties. The only third-party services used are:

  • Google AdMob (advertising, free tier only)
  • Sentry (anonymous crash reporting)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management, no personal data shared)

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in the app's Settings screen. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
